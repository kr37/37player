Phones and tablets

### iPad and iPhone: start from the Home Screen

Safari on an iPad or iPhone erases a website's data after 7 days without a visit, and the library with it. A 37 Player added to the Home Screen keeps its library. It's a separate app with a library of its own, so do this **before** importing anything:

1. In Safari, open 37 Player and tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up), then **View More**. If Share isn't showing, tap **•••** first.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**. Leave **Open as Web App** turned on: turned off, the icon only opens the website in Safari, with Safari's library and its 7-day limit. Then tap **Add**.
3. Open **37 Player** from the Home Screen, and set it up there.

Already built a library in Safari? Use 'Import / Export' → Export there, then Import in the Home Screen app.

### Android: install it as an app

In Chrome on Android, 37 Player offers to install itself when it first opens; otherwise use Chrome's ⋮ menu → **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**. The installed app and the website share one library, and Chrome keeps an installed app's data safe rather than clearing it when space runs low.

### Touch

On a touch screen, 'Edit Playlists' and the 'Library' work by tapping and holding instead of clicking and dragging.

### Selecting

- **Tap** a track to select it. Its ✎ and ✕ show once it's selected.
- **Hold, then slide** up or down to select a run of tracks.
- **Tap a folder** to open or close it. Its Print, ✎ and ✕ show on the folder you tapped last.

### Moving

- **Tracks:** select them, then hold a selected track until it lifts (about half a second) and slide it to where it should go. From the right-hand column, this copies tracks into the playlist being edited.
- **Folders and playlists** in the first column: hold one until it lifts, then slide it onto a folder to put it inside, or between two rows to move it there. The bar under the list moves it to the very end.
- Near the top or bottom edge, the column scrolls by itself.

Copy and paste work too, from the bar above each list.

### Column widths

On a tablet, drag the thin line between two columns to make a column wider, such as the right-hand one, so long titles can be read. Turning on **Wrap** in the bar along the top of 'Playing' (or 'Library' → Table) shows titles in full on every row.

On a phone, the screen slides sideways between the columns. Drag the thin line at a column's right-hand edge with a finger to make that column wider or narrower (up to nine tenths of the screen, so its line always shows); the columns to its right just move along. The widths are kept for next time, separately for holding the phone upright and on its side. To put every column back as it started, use **Reset column widths** at the bottom of Display settings (◐).

### Playing with the screen off

A playlist keeps playing with the screen locked or while you use another app, and moves from track to track by itself. While the screen is off, 37 Player also plays a silent sound alongside the track. Without it, Android lets a track that starts with the screen off play for a couple of seconds and then silences it.

If playback ever goes quiet while the time keeps moving, open **Settings → Playback → Playback log** afterwards. It records when each track started, when the app left and came back to the screen, and whether the phone stopped or paused anything. **Copy all** puts the whole log on the clipboard, and **Share…** sends it as a text file (on a computer without sharing, **Save as a file** saves it): both start with this device's version, browser and storage, so whoever helps you can tell where it came from. That's easier and safer than selecting lines by hand, which can leave an older copy on the clipboard. Or use **Send feedback…** in Help to send the log to the author of 37 Player with a note of what happened.

At a stop, the lock screen keeps showing the stop's name with a ▶. Pressing it, there or on headphones, continues the session without unlocking. The same after pausing with the screen locked: the paused track stays on the lock screen, and ▶ carries on from where it was. For that, 37 Player plays silence while it waits: through a stop for as long as it lasts, after a pause for up to 30 minutes. That uses a little battery. To save battery, turn off **Keep the lock screen ready at stops and pauses** in Settings → Playback; then carry on from 37 Player itself.
