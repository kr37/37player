37 Player: Privacy

37 Player is a music player and playlist organizer that runs in your web browser. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, sets no cookies, and sells nothing to anyone. This page says where your information goes when you use it.

Your library stays on your device

Your tracks, playlists and settings are stored by your browser, on your own device. They aren't sent anywhere unless you choose one of the things below.

This website

37player.com serves the app itself. Like most web servers, it keeps ordinary access logs (the address a request came from, the time, and the page asked for) for a limited time, to keep the site running. They aren't used for anything else.

Shared libraries on 37player.com

If you join or start a shared library kept on 37player.com, that library's tracks, playlists and the names of the devices in it are stored on this server, so that the devices sharing it can keep each other up to date. Anyone with the library's join code can open it. Leaving the library on your device doesn't remove it from the server; to have a shared library removed, contact us (below).

Shared libraries on Google Drive

If you choose to keep a shared library in Google Drive, 37 Player asks Google for permission to see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app (Google's drive.file access). With it, 37 Player can see only the folder and files it made itself. It can't see anything else in your Drive.

  • Your library's tracks and playlists go directly between your device and your Google Drive. They never pass through 37player.com.
  • Signing in passes once through a small helper on 37player.com, which trades Google's one-time sign-in code for the keys your device needs to stay signed in. The helper stores nothing and logs nothing. The keys are kept only on your device.
  • 37 Player uses this access only to keep your shared library in sync. It doesn't share your Google data with anyone, use it for advertising, or use it for anything else.
  • You can remove 37 Player's access at any time in your Google Account, at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The library's folder stays in your Drive until you delete it.

37 Player's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Converting audio

When 37 Player converts a file to another format, it does so on your device, with a converter downloaded once from 37player.com. Your audio isn't uploaded to be converted.

Contact
